# Fairbuild pricing (ver 1)

The software is free (MIT). You pay a fixed price for the setup: we install the agents on your computer, map your exports (Procore, Sage 100 or 300 Contractor, Foundation, Vista, QuickBooks, Buildertrend, ServiceTitan or your spreadsheets), set your rules (retainage rates, notice days, insurance requirements, wage and fringe rates, tax rates), run the first reports with you and train the people who will use them.

These prices are proposals for Kurt to confirm. They sit above the restaurant family (Fairtable) because a contractor's back office handles more money per transaction, more parties per job and more rules per project.

## Support after setup

- $200 a month for one pack
- $600 a month for the whole back office
- $250 a month per company for partner rollouts

Support covers changes in your exports (Procore or your accounting system changing its columns), rule and rate updates you ask for (a new wage determination, a new retainage rate, new insurance requirements), new versions of the agents and a monthly check-in call.

## Included and not included

Included: installation on your computer, mapping your exports, your settings, the first run of every agent in the package with you, training, the first reports.

Not included: paying anyone, filing or releasing a lien, submitting or approving a pay application, posting to your ledger, running or submitting payroll, requesting bonds, or sending any message (the agents never do these; a person on your team does). Legal, tax, lien, insurance, surety or accounting advice. Logging in to your accounting or project management system, bank, surety, insurer or any government portal.

Your files and reports stay on your computer. Worker names, worker ids, Social Security numbers and homeowner contacts are masked in anything the agents send to an AI model.
