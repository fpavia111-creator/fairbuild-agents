# Yukon fixtures

Everything here is fictional: locals, funds, workers and rates.

- `hours.csv`: October hours by worker for three locals (Local 100: 500 hours; Local 200: 310; Local 300: 80).
- `rates.csv`: contribution rates for Local 100 and Local 200; Local 300 has none.
- `remittances.csv`: Local 100 Pension was paid on 480 hours (125 short); Local 100 Training was not paid; Local 200 Health and Welfare was paid on the 18th, after the 15th; Local 200 Pension was paid 50 over.
