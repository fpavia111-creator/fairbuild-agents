# Seine fixtures

Everything here is fictional: jobs 24-101 (Example Clinic fit-out) and 24-102 (Sample Library roof), their schedule of values lines and amounts.

- `sov.csv`: billing to date and field percent per line. Drywall (24-101 line 4) and doors (line 6) are billed ahead of the field; electrical (line 5) and roofing (24-102 line 2) are billed behind it; cleanup (24-102 line 4) is billed on a zero-value line with no field percent.
- `jobs.csv`: the revised contract of 24-101 is 455,000, but its schedule of values adds up to 450,000.
- `change-orders.csv`: CO-001 is approved and has no line on the schedule of values.
