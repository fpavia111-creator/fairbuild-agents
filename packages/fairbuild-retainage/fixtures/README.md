# Fairbuild Retainage fixtures

Everything here is fictional: jobs, owners (Example Health Partners, Sample County Library, Example School District, Sample Retail LLC, Example Clinic Annex, Sample Parks Department), subcontractors and amounts.

- `receivable.csv`: six jobs. 24-102 records 4,000 less retainage than its rate; 23-090 finished in August and its 15,000 has not been billed for release; 23-095 billed its release in June and has not collected it; on 24-103 retainage stops at 50% of the contract and the owner holds 5,000 too much; 24-101 and 22-050 are clean.
- `payable.csv`: five subcontracts. Example Storefronts was released 6,000 before the owner paid on 23-095; Sample Roofing is held at 7% instead of 5%; the owner paid 22-050 in full, so Example Paving is now owed its 8,000.
