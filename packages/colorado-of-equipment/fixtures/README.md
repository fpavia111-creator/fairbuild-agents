# Colorado fixtures

Everything here is fictional: rental vendors (Sample Equipment Rentals, Example Lift Rentals), machines, rates and hours.

- `rentals.csv`: six rental invoices. The excavator (RI-9001) was billed four weeks at 6,400 when one month costs 4,800; the scissor lift (RI-9002) was called off on 1 November and billed through the 11th; the light tower (RI-9003) logged no hours; the compactor's second invoice (RI-9005) bills 10 to 14 November again; the skid steer (RI-9006) has no job.
- `usage.csv`: hours by machine and day from daily reports.
