# Brazos fixtures

Everything here is fictional: jobs, subcontractors, backcharges and balances.

- `backcharges.csv`: six backcharges. BC-01 was agreed in October and never deducted; BC-02's notice went out 16 days after the work; BC-03 is disputed and the roofer has been paid in full; BC-04 was deducted at 1,500 against 1,800; BC-05 has no backup; BC-06 is closed.
- `balances.csv`: what is still owed or held on each subcontractor.
