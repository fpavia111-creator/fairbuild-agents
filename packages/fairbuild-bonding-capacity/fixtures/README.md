# Fairbuild Bonding Capacity fixtures

Everything here is fictional: jobs, limits, working capital and bids. The limits are made up for the test and are not advice.

- `jobs.csv`: six jobs, four bonded. 24-301 is a 2.6 million bonded contract against a 2.5 million single-job limit; 24-103 is a bonded job forecast at a loss.
- `limits.json`: single 2.5 million, aggregate 4 million, flag at 70% used.
- `pipeline.csv`: two bonded bids that together would take bonded backlog past the aggregate limit.
